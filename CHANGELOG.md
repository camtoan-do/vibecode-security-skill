# Changelog

What changed in this repo, newest first, in plain English.

## 2026-11-09

### Added
- **`launch-readiness` skill.** Audits a site for the 19 launch basics: custom 404 page, CTA above the fold, meta title and description per page, Open Graph image, favicon, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, alt text, mobile breakpoint, sticky mobile CTA, loading states, form error states, thank-you page, privacy policy page, terms page, cookie banner, analytics, and a real contact address. Each item has a security and resilience check next to the polish one. The three legal items hand off to `legal-readiness`.
- **Canada pack for `legal-readiness`.** What each legal document must include and how to lower liability under PIPEDA, provincial privacy laws including Quebec Law 25, CASL, and consumer protection rules. United States and European Union / UK packs are marked as underway.
- **`legal-readiness` skill.** Audits an app for the 14 legal and privacy basics and puts the missing pieces in place.
